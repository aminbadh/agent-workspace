# The Compass

**The arbitration layer.** Every other file here describes what is true inside one domain. This one ranks the areas of the user's life against each other, the one question none of them can answer. It sits at the root beside [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), not inside a domain, because it has authority over all of them.

## How it works

Adapted from Gary Keller's *The ONE Thing*: success is sequential, not simultaneous. In each area there is one next thing that makes the rest easier or unnecessary. Every rung of a ladder exists only to make the rung below it obvious.

> **The focusing question:** what is the ONE Thing I can do right now, such that by doing it, everything else will be easier or unnecessary?

The areas are the user's own choice, picked at onboarding from the Wheel of Life and renamed or merged as they like. The Monthly rung is each area's lead domino. The week's plan is drawn from the Monthly rungs in the weekly review and goes to the task home; it is not stored here. Which area to act on right now is not stored either, because it depends on what is blocked, what just opened and what is closing soon: the one-thing skill derives it.

## Keeping it honest

- **Rungs link, they do not restate.** A rung that names a deadline points to the domain or the task home where the date lives.
- **Rungs are goals, not notes.** Reasoning belongs in the domain's DECISIONS.md.
- **A rung marked *(proposed)*** was suggested by an agent and is the user's to confirm.
- **When a session closes something a rung names,** update the rung and bump `last_reviewed` in the same session.
