# Newsletter: Decisions

*Part of the invented example. Append-only: entries are added and superseded, never deleted.*

## Open
- **Whether to add a paid tier.** Kept open until there are 500 subscribers, because below that
  even a strong conversion rate would not cover the tool's cost. What would settle it: five
  readers saying unprompted that they would pay.

## Closed Decisions
- **Weekly on Tuesdays, 12 Sep 2026.** Weekend issues opened at half the rate, and Tuesday
  beat Monday in a four-week test.
- **One idea per issue, 20 Aug 2026.** The long issues had the lowest click rate and the most
  unsubscribes.
- **Stay on Buttondown's paid plan past 100 subscribers, 5 Sep 2026.** Moving the list
  would cost more time than the plan costs money.
- **Send from Buttondown's free plan, 1 Aug 2026.** Markdown in, no tracking pixels by
  default. **Superseded 5 Sep 2026 by:** the paid-plan entry above, once the list passed 100.

## Key Learnings & Principles
- **Write the subject line first.** Every issue that started from the subject was tighter than
  the ones given a subject at the end.
- **The reply matters more than the post.** Most sign-ups came from answering questions in the
  community, not from the monthly post itself.
