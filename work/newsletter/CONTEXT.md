# Domain: Newsletter

*An invented example showing the shape of a domain. Copy it, then delete it.*

## Overview

A weekly email for early-career product managers: one practical idea per issue, short enough
to read between meetings. Started as a side project to practise writing in public; it may
become a paid product if it finds readers.

**The current picture:** 14 issues sent, around 300 subscribers, most of them from one Slack
community. Open rates hold near 50%, and that community is the only channel that works so far.

## Status & Priorities

### Active Workstreams
- **A second channel.** Testing short posts on LinkedIn that link back to the archive. Tasks
  in the task app's Newsletter project.

### Next Up
- **Decide the paid tier**, once there are 500 subscribers. The question and what would
  settle it are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

## Operating Context

### Constraints & Boundaries
- Two hours a week, Sunday mornings. Anything that needs more waits.
- No affiliate links: readers have to trust that nothing is being sold to them.

### Key People & Orgs
