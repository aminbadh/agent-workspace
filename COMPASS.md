# The Compass

**The arbitration layer.** Every other file here describes what is true inside one domain.
This one ranks the areas of your life against each other, the one question none of them can
answer. It sits beside [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md), not
inside a domain, because it has authority over all of them.

## How it works

Adapted from Gary Keller's *The ONE Thing*: success is sequential, not simultaneous. In each
area there is one next thing that makes the rest easier or unnecessary. Every rung of the
ladder exists only to make the rung below it obvious.

> **The focusing question:** what is the ONE Thing I can do right now, such that by doing it,
> everything else will be easier or unnecessary?

The areas below come from the Wheel of Life. Rename, merge or drop them; five is plenty.
**Scores** are a quick self-assessment out of 5 (3 is holding, 5 needs nothing from you right
now). They measure health, not priority: a low score flags a starved area, it does not win.

## The Board

One ONE Thing per area. **Strike it through when it is done**, and replace it the next time
you work that area.

*Which* area to act on right now is not stored here, because it depends on what is blocked,
what just opened and what is closing soon. Ask your agent to derive it (see
[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md#the-compass)).

## Keeping it honest

- **Rungs link, they do not restate.** A rung that names a deadline points to the domain or the
  task app where the date lives.
- **Rungs are goals, not notes.** Reasoning belongs in the domain's `DECISIONS.md`.
- **Mark a rung *(proposed)*** when an agent suggested it and you have not confirmed it yet.
